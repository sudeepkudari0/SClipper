# Naya Yeira — *Almost There* (EP) Campaign

Campaign profile for the **Naya Yeira — Almost There (EP)** TikTok + Instagram Reels creator campaign.

## Key Rules Encoded in `profile.yaml`

- **Audio**: `output.audio: mute` and `bans.bakedMusic: true`. **Never** bake the song into the `.mp4` — always attach the official TikTok or Instagram sound inside the app.
- **Duration & Drop Timing**: Minimum `15s` (`minSeconds: 15`), with `dropAtSec: 3.0` so the 0–3s hook leads into the visual reveal at ~3s and at least 12s of the song plays afterward.
- **Required Credit**: `Hit Record — Naya Yeira` (edit `captions.requiredPhrases` in `profile.yaml` if you pick a different song from *Almost There*).
- **Platform Mentions**:
  - TikTok: `@nayayeiraofficial`
  - Instagram Reels: `@nayayeira`
- **No AI-generated footage/voiceover** (`bans.aiFootage: true`, `bans.aiVoiceover: true`).

## Rules the Tool Cannot Verify

- **Official in-app audio**: You must manually select the official track sound inside TikTok / Instagram (never upload as original audio).
- **Originality & creative fit**: Avoid interchangeable background footage; make sure your visual concept or artist edit genuinely connects to the song.
- **Organic traffic & 1% like-to-view ratio**: No boosted/paid/bot traffic; supply analytics if requested during review.
